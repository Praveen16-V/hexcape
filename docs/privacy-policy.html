Privacy Policy for Hexcape

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Hexcape does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Everything the game knows about you stays on your own device.

This policy explains what that means in practice for the Hexcape Android application (the “app”), published on Google Play under the package name com.hexcape.hexcape.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it has no way to send such information anywhere even if it had it.

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, storage, or contacts.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game state locally so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your phone. It consists of:

  • Which levels you have completed and the stars you earned on them.
  • Your settings — volume, difficulty, vibration, reduced motion, and hints.
  • Whether you have purchased the full game.
  • Which cosmetic pets you have unlocked.

All of it is held in Android’s standard app preferences storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also clear it at any time from your device’s Settings → Apps → Hexcape → Storage → Clear data.

Purchases

Hexcape offers a single optional one-time purchase that unlocks the levels beyond the free ones. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details — not your card number, your billing address, or your name.

This is the only situation in which the app communicates over the network, and that communication is between your device and Google. Google’s handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you reinstall the app or install it on another device, the app asks Google Play whether this Google account already owns the purchase, so that you do not pay twice. It receives only a yes or no.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not read the Android Advertising ID and does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing your information

We have no information about you to share, and we share none. No data is sold, rented, or transferred to third parties.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us — though as described above, there is no mechanism by which this could occur.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access or correct. To delete the game data stored on your own device, uninstall the app or clear its data as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about the app can be sent to toolsilahub@gmail.com.
